# 📖 PocketPages

A warm, cozy personal sanctuary for storing daily moments, cherished memories, and birthdays. Crafted with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Cozy & Warm Aesthetic**: Designed with warm terracotta, honey amber, dusky rose, parchment cream, and soft paper textures.
- **Save & Edit Timestamps**:
  - Every note visibly tracks **"Saved on: [Date & Time]"** and **"Edited on: [Date & Time]"** (along with relative time e.g. *5m ago*, *yesterday*).
  - The editor displays when the note was originally created and when it was last edited.
- **Categorization**:
  - 📖 **Daily Moments**: Quick thoughts, daily highlights, and journal reflections.
  - 💭 **Memories**: Stories, keepsakes, and nostalgia.
  - 🎂 **Birthdays**: Built-in countdown engine that shows how many days remain until their next birthday and what age they are turning!
  - 📝 **General Notes**: Ideas, lists, and recipes.
- **Multi-Device Mobile Access**:
  - **Google / Gmail Sign-In**: 1-click **Continue with Google (Gmail)** to sign in with your Gmail address on any mobile device or PC.
  - **Account Credentials**: Register and sign in with email and password so each user has their own private journal.
  - **Fresh Canvas for Every User**: New accounts always begin completely clean and empty, ready for you to add your own pages.
  - **Wi-Fi Mobile Access**: Run `serve.bat` to instantly open PocketPages on your phone's browser (Safari/Chrome) over your home Wi-Fi.
  - **Backup & Instant Transfer**: 1-click JSON export & import to move your notes between your computer and mobile phone.
  - **Cloud Hosting Ready**: 100% static client-side web app — can be deployed for free to GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify in 1 minute.
- **Search, Filter & Sort**:
  - Instant live keyword search.
  - Category filters with real-time counters.
  - Sort by Newest Created, Recently Edited, Upcoming Birthdays, or Oldest.
- **Mobile First & PWA**:
  - Mobile Floating Action Button (`+ New Page`).
  - Add to Home Screen support (`manifest.json`).

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Double-Click (Easiest)
Simply double-click **`serve.bat`** in this folder!
A console window will open displaying:
- **PC Browser**: `http://localhost:8080/`
- **Mobile Browser**: `http://<Your-PC-IP>:8080/` (open this URL on your phone connected to the same Wi-Fi!)

### Option 2: Open Directly
You can also directly double-click **`index.html`** in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

---

## 📱 How to Access on Another Mobile Device

### Method A: Over Local Wi-Fi (No Internet Required)
1. Double-click `serve.bat` on your PC.
2. Note the mobile address shown (for example: `http://192.168.1.15:8080`).
3. Connect your mobile phone to the same Wi-Fi network.
4. Open Chrome or Safari on your phone and type that address.
5. Log in with your credentials!

### Method B: Free Online Cloud Deployment (Worldwide Access)
Because PocketPages is built purely with HTML, CSS, and JS:
1. Upload this folder to a GitHub repository or drop the folder onto [Vercel](https://vercel.com) or [Netlify](https://netlify.com).
2. You will get a free permanent URL (like `https://pocketpages.vercel.app`).
3. Open it on your phone anytime, tap "Add to Home Screen", and enjoy your pocket journal anywhere!
